Debt snowball vs avalanche calculator

List what you owe, add the extra you can pay each month, and see both methods side by side: the date you are debt-free, the interest you pay in total, and the month each debt disappears. The full monthly plan downloads as a CSV that opens in Excel or Google Sheets.

How the numbers are worked out

Each month, interest is added to every balance at APR / 12. Every debt receives its minimum payment. Your extra, plus the minimum of every debt already paid off (the "snowball"), goes to the target debt: the smallest balance for the snowball, the highest APR for the avalanche. When the target is cleared mid-month, the leftover rolls straight to the next one. The total you pay each month never changes, which is what makes the method work.

Snowball or avalanche?

The avalanche always costs the same or less interest, and the box above shows by how much in your case. The snowball clears a first debt sooner, which is the reason many people stick with it. If the gap shown is small, take the snowball. If it is large, the avalanche pays you to be patient.

Limits

Fixed APR, payments at the start of each month, no new charges, no fees and no promotional 0% periods. Card issuers compound daily, so the real interest is a little higher. If a minimum payment does not cover a debt's monthly interest, the calculator says so. Nothing you type leaves this page.
